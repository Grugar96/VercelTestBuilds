Before you come

Questions we get a lot

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. Most of our guests have never stood on a board. Every session starts with theory on the sand and beach drills before you go in.

Do I need to know how to swim?

Being comfortable in the water helps. Beginner sessions stay in shallow whitewash with an instructor next to you. If you can't swim, tell us when you book and we'll plan around it.

When is the best time to surf in Mulki?

The surf season runs from September to May. Mornings usually have the cleanest conditions, which is why sessions run from 8 to 11 AM.

What's included in a surf lesson?

A soft-top surfboard, a rash guard, an instructor, theory and beach drills, then time in the water.
