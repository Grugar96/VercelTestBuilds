Before you come

Good to know

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 1:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. Arriving early? Tell us and we'll keep your bags safe.

Can I work remotely from here?

Yes. There's Wi-Fi at the hostel, and afternoons are quiet once the morning session is done.

When is the surf season?

September to May. June to August is monsoon, when the sea is too rough for lessons.

Do I pay online?

No. You send a booking request, and we confirm availability and the final price with you on WhatsApp.
