How a lesson works

From the sand to standing up

  1. Theory on the sand

    Waves, safety and where to sit, drawn right there in the sand.

  2. Beach drills

    Paddling and the pop-up, practised on the board before you get wet.

  3. Into the whitewash

    Your instructor helps you into broken waves until you're standing.

  4. Green waves

    On longer programs you paddle out and learn to read and ride unbroken waves.

Sessions run in the morning, between 8 and 11 AM. Exact start times follow the tide and are confirmed on WhatsApp the evening before.
