About us

A community,not a holiday ad.

Mulki Surf Brothers is a surf school and riverside hostel where the river meets the ocean. Come for a session, stay a little longer, leave with stories.

Learn to surf. Stay a little longer. Make friends. Explore Mulki. Leave with stories.

We teach beginners on a gentle, sandy beach, and we keep the vibe the way we like it: patient in the water, loud at dinner.

Nobody gets shouted at for falling off. Everybody falls off.
