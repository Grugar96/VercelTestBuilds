Who We Are

From a regional enterprise to a global integrated commodity group.

Incorporated in Chennai in 1990, Sri Priyanka Geo Commex Limited has grown from a regional trading enterprise into a globally diversified, commodity-focused group — supplying critical minerals and manufacturing rice bran oil across India, Singapore and Morocco.

The group holds mining licences for the commercial exploitation of its barite and copper mines in Morocco, the world’s third-largest barite producer, and operates its own crude and refined rice bran oil manufacturing in Andhra Pradesh. Between the pit and the port, SPGCL controls the chain it sells into — which is what allows it to guarantee origin, grade and delivery to industrial buyers across more than a dozen markets.

  • Traceable Sourcing

    Owned mining licences and long-standing supplier relationships give every consignment a documented origin.

  • In-House Refining

    Group-owned processing converts crude feedstock into refined rice bran oil and saleable by-products.

  • Long-Term Partnerships

    Repeat industrial offtake across Asia, the Gulf and North Africa, served through subsidiaries in Singapore and Morocco.

Our Vision for the Future

Two divisions, one integrated global enterprise.

SPGCL is building toward a group in which consumer brands and industrial supply share the same resource base — a business that takes what it mines and refines all the way through to the shelf, and sells the balance into the world’s industrial markets.

Consumer FMCG

Branded edible oils and packaged goods built on group-owned refining, taking SPGCL products directly to household consumers.

B2B Commodities

Critical minerals and bulk agro consignments supplied under long-term offtake to industrial buyers across more than twelve markets.

Linking natural resources to everyday life — from the mine face and the mill, to the port, to the shelf.
